## Contract

doctor schema/枚举采用ai-native-delivery.md第4节。配置仅映射六个已知ID到绝对executable路径；拒绝未知字段、相对路径、目录、非执行文件和参数字符串。显式配置错误不回退PATH。仅固定版本参数，5秒超时、单流64KiB上限，禁止子命令help隐式启动服务。输出不含主目录绝对路径/认证/环境信息，executable显示basename。能力按证据标verified/unverified/unsupported，版本不认证。

See docs/engineering/ai-native-delivery.md for state, authorization, idempotency and trust boundaries. Product accounting, data schema and personal MCP are outside scope. Shared wiring is integrator-owned.
