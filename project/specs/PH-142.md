### `docs/v1.0-development/contracts/entities.schema.json#/$defs/SourceConsumption`

- 对象 必填：organization_id, source, consumer, coverage_key, amount_cents, event_ref, id, version
- organization_id 引用：./contract-bundle.schema.json#/$defs/Id
- source 引用：./contract-bundle.schema.json#/$defs/SourceRef
- consumer 枚举：["cost", "management_revenue", "obligation", "clearance", "invoice_allocation", "forecast", "opening"]
- amount_cents 引用：./contract-bundle.schema.json#/$defs/CnyCents
- id 引用：./contract-bundle.schema.json#/$defs/Id
- version 固定：1

此命令必须实现的约束（含拒绝路径）：

- Coverage key comes from validated business range/period/line, never an arbitrary caller nonce.
- Versions resolve immutable inputs; changed versions do not permit full reposting. Corrections append linked delta/reversal and coverage release.
- Stable id/version identifies the immutable original claim. Effective consumed/released coverage is derived from linked append-only accounting events; an adjustment reuses original claim and cannot create a second claim for the same business key.

查询使用queries与同名query-results；命令回执使用command-results同名定义；事件只追加events的具名版本。来源namespace/kind/owner按source-registry，关系事实/投影归属按relationships，严禁另建第二份可编辑余额。补充契约已合入canonical定义时按canonical执行，不继承source_excerpt中的旧GAP结论；未冻结字段仍须规划解决。

## 状态、幂等与拒绝场景

本片状态推进只限上述行为，实体枚举及服务约束严格取schema_refs；无写行为的查询/验证片不新增状态机。对草稿、批准版本、已过账事实和读投影分别处理；批准后改稿重新审批，已过账只追加冲销/开放期调整。不得以schema校验通过替代服务授权与累计核算。
共同拒绝：跨企业/项目、无权/撤权、模块停用、过期版本、重复原来源消费、自审、未来实际日期、锁期、超核销/分配覆盖、未知政策及缺证据；只对本片适用业务入口执行，不给纯读取凭空增加记账。每个拒绝断言响应及业务/账本/审计/Outbox无半笔新增。金额整数分、分钟整数，bool/float拒绝；Decimal/ROUND_HALF_UP，金额绝对值≤10^12分。
来源消费键基于企业+原稳定来源+用途/业务覆盖，排除可变版本/request key/调用渠道；版本仍用于不可变快照读取。同key同输入复用结果，改输入冲突；unknown outcome先查结果，不盲重放。PostgreSQL按company→period→project→sources→cash→open_item→allocation稳定ID序加锁，事实/桥/账本/审计/Outbox同事务。dry_run默认服务器true，零持久副作用；AI建议不构成审批授权。
