Unknown stays null. Confirming invoice face tax or supplier metadata does not itself change posted expense; tax deductibility/cost delta uses existing TaxQualification and approved open-period correction.
- v1 supports one complete fee per original bank cash source only. Unique (company, original bank source identity) excludes BankCharge ID, amount, date, evidence changes, method/version and request key; a changed draft revises the same charge, not another charge. Cost and fee-settlement use distinct purpose keys on that same original bank identity. Multiple fee lines require later explicit planning.

- Existing cost basis must be expense-kind: CostEvent.cost_kind=expense or an effective legacy cost posting resolving to an approved expense document. Salary/labor, shared allocation, vendor acceptance, prepayment consumption and cash-book rows are not eligible even if amounts happen to match.
- An aliased CostEvent must resolve through its actual typed source to an ExpenseClaim or approved legacy expense, never back to this or another BankCharge. Reject cyclic/self fee-cost aliases. A directly posted BankCharge uses existing_cost_ref=null and its normal source posting path.
- One effective fee-cost binding per original cost identity and original bank fee identity. Existing expense/cost and bank-charge aliases share company-cost consumption; do not post through BankCharge when linked. Never copy an existing expense into another cost, edit historical cents, fake a new AccountingEvent for legacy, or permit later cost reversal while an effective fee settlement depends on it.
- v1 existing-cost reuse requires verified complete obligation/payment coverage: no outstanding AP from that same normalized expense/fee origin and no separate cash/advance/other funding payment of that same fee. Unknown coverage is policy_unconfirmed, never assumed zero. An expense with unpaid AP or a fee paid separately rejects this net-withholding settlement route; do not erase AP, invent cash clearance or introduce an unapproved AP offset.
