此命令必须实现的约束（含拒绝路径）：

- Approve verified salary components/total once as company cost, initially unallocated; missing capacity blocks project distribution, not preservation of known wage facts.
- allocation_inputs is null until complete server snapshot; project totals disclose unallocated coverage instead of claiming complete cost.
- Components in immutable allocation snapshot are resolved from original pool version; no separate editable total/policy.
- A planned full-month salary pool is an approved rate/forecast basis, not a full-month company cost posting.
- Only incurred amounts for confirmed coverage through <=current business date can be company cost; disclose provisional versus reconciled actual.
- Interim approved-minute estimates and final payroll use the same organization/employee/month labor origin. Final adjustment posts target cumulative cost minus already-effective cost, never adds full payroll on top.

来源消费键基于企业+原稳定来源+用途/业务覆盖，排除可变版本/request key/调用渠道；版本仍用于不可变快照读取。同key同输入复用结果，改输入冲突；unknown outcome先查结果，不盲重放。PostgreSQL按company→period→project→sources→cash→open_item→allocation稳定ID序加锁，事实/桥/账本/审计/Outbox同事务。dry_run默认服务器true，零持久副作用；AI建议不构成审批授权。
