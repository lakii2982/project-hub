### 2. `docs/v1.0-development/contracts/entities.schema.json`（冻结精确替换句）

仅修订 `SourceConsumption.x-service-invariants`：把**第 6 项（0-based index 5，当前以 “The original bank_effect claim is permanent.” 开头的那一项）整体、逐字替换为下列冻结句**；其余 7 项逐字保留，`consumer` 枚举 8 值（含 `cash`）不得删改，其它结构/约束不得放松：

```text
The original bank_effect claim is permanent. Each effective classified bank fact has exactly one effective original cash-book posting group: one AccountingEvent and one LedgerPosting with book=cash_in for an incoming cash transaction or cash_out for an outgoing one, origin=current, and scope.kind=funding_account pointing at the original FundingAccount; at any time a bank fact has at most one effective original posting group and no second effective group may represent the same bank cash. Classification reversal appends a mirror reversal AccountingEvent (reverses_event_ref pointing at the original event) and an equal negative LedgerPosting (reverses_posting_id pointing at the original posting) with book/scope identical to the original; it never creates a second positive bank flow. After a complete classification reversal, reclassification may append a new original posting group and must reuse the same bank_effect claim; it cannot create two concurrently effective original groups, cannot release the claim and cannot create or copy a second bank_effect claim. Cash allocation and its release create no AccountingEvent and no LedgerPosting. The bank_effect claim is permanent and classification, allocation, reversal and release never release bank_effect. Project allocation never reposts bank cash, and cash consumption is not clearance consumption. The consumer enum retains cost, management_revenue, obligation, clearance, invoice_allocation, forecast, opening and cash.
```

该句须同时覆盖：①每条**有效**分类恰一组原始现金账 posting group（book=cash_in/out、scope.kind=funding_account、origin=current），且**任一时刻同一 bank fact 至多一组有效原始组**；②撤分类只追加镜像冲销事件（`reverses_event_ref`）与等额负值 posting（`reverses_posting_id`），不产生第二笔正向银行流；③完全撤分类后**重新分类可追加新的原始 posting 组并复用同一 bank_effect claim**，不得出现第二组并发有效组、不得释放或复制 claim；④allocation 与 release 明确零 AccountingEvent/零 LedgerPosting；⑤保留既有短语与 8 值 consumer 枚举、不删既有 consumer。与 PH-239 spec 的“撤后重新分类复用同一 claim、任一时刻至多一组有效原始组”交叉一致。
