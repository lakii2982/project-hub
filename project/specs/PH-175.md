此命令必须实现的约束（含拒绝路径）：

- Authenticated company/member/project, company-first lock, enabled owner, expected version, period, stable original source, idempotency and full rollback required.
- No PAT/MCP/AI approval, payment initiation or closing tools. Server derives actor/company; dry_run persists no facts, ledger, audit, outbox or idempotency result.
- cash application orchestrates, settlement alone owns ReceiptFeeSettlement relation. Actual net bank CashTransaction and ledger remain unchanged; no synthetic gross cash or fee cash is created.
- Requires net incoming same-company cash, receivable same counterparty/project, approved company-borne charge and explicit evidence of gross payer payment and withheld fee. Mere net receipt is insufficient.
- Resolve BankCharge.cash_transaction_ref and input cash_transaction_ref to original bank identities and require exact equality, not just company/project/counterparty similarity. Existing net clearance must be effective and not reversed; fee cost must currently be posted and not reversed/superseded, not merely posted in history.
- Require exactly one unreversed application per original bank charge under v1; server computes applied_cents from approved gross fee, never accepts client balance or fee-settlement amount. This v1 does not support arbitrary partial/split or multiple-open-item fee application; report the limitation rather than guessing.
- If existing_net_clearance_ref supplied, verify it connects exactly this net cash and open item and equals the actual net receipt; do not clear again. Otherwise ordinary clearance consumes only actual net cash via unchanged FundingAvailabilityProof in same lock/transaction.
- First-use target open remaining must equal net actual receipt plus fee, or after exact verified prior net clearance must equal fee. Any mismatch, prior unrelated consumption, partial availability or unsupported combination rejects all changes.
- Resolve fee cost basis either from the approved BankCharge source posting or its explicitly approved existing_cost_ref alias, never both. It must currently be effective exactly once with verified same-bank fee gross/tax linkage, not reversed. No extra cost/AR/right/revenue/cash entry. Noncash fee settlement is not a discount, bad debt, advance or a funding availability proof.
- Recheck same-origin AP/payment coverage at settlement time under company lock. For linked existing costs, outstanding AP or a prior separate fee payment/advance offset blocks this route; incomplete legacy obligation coverage blocks with policy_unconfirmed. No AP is deleted or silently cleared. Wrong expense classification must be corrected through its existing approved expense+obligation reversal workflow before a new direct bank-charge source is posted.
- BankCharge is one source for company expense and a separate one-time settlement purpose; amount/version/channel changes cannot reset either source consumption. Rollback joins clearance, bridge, consumption, audit/outbox atomically.
- Reverse dependency is enforced at every service entry: while this receipt-fee settlement is effective, net clearance and supporting fee cost cannot be individually reversed/replaced/voided. First explicitly reverse the fee-settlement bridge in an open period, then correct its dependencies. No silent cascade creates refunds or alters cash/cost.
